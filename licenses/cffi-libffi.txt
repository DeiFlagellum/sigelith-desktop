libffi inside cffi's _cffi_backend.pyd
======================================

The Windows build of cffi 2.1.1 does not link a libffi library: its setup.py
compiles the copy of libffi 2.00-beta that cffi carries in
src/c/libffi_x86_x64 ("from the CPython distribution", says the setup.py)
into _internal/_cffi_backend.cp314-win_amd64.pyd, which accordingly imports
no libffi DLL.  On 64-bit Windows the files compiled in are ffi.c, prep_cif.c
and types.c, with the assembler routines of win64.obj.  The headers of those
sources carry these copyright notices:
